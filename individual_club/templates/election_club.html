How to Vote & Security Information

Voting Guide: How to Participate in Club Officer Elections
  1. Confirm Eligibility: Ensure you are an active member of the club.
  2. Click “Vote Now”: Locate the blue “Vote Now” button at the bottom of the page.
  3. Review Candidates & Submit: Select your preferred candidate(s). Click “Submit Vote”.
  4. Print Your Receipt (Optional): Click “Print Vote Slip” for a timestamped receipt.

This receipt does NOT reveal your vote — it only confirms participation.

Security & Privacy
  • What Your Printout Contains: Vote transaction ID, Timestamp, Club name, Election title.
  • What It Does NOT Contain: Your selected candidate(s) or personal info beyond member ID.
  • Why We Hide Vote Details: To ensure ballot secrecy.
Pro Tips
  • Vote from a private device.
  • Never share credentials.
  • Keep your receipt safe.
