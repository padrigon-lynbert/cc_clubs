Boxing Club Annual Championship: School Boxing Match

Event Overview

Get ready for an exciting night of action, skill, and sportsmanship as the BCP Boxing Club presents its Annual Championship Boxing Match! Join us for an evening filled with energy and competitive spirit as our talented student boxers face off in the ring. This is a fantastic opportunity to witness firsthand the hard work, dedication, and training that our athletes have put in throughout the year.

Whether you're a boxing enthusiast or new to the sport, this event will keep you on the edge of your seat. Don't miss the chance to support your fellow students and cheer for your favorite boxers!

Event Schedule

  • 6:00 PM – 6:30 PM: Opening Ceremony – Welcome speech by the Boxing Club President, introduction to the event, and brief explanation of the rules and matchups. A special appearance by the school principal to kick off the event.
  • 6:30 PM – 7:15 PM: First Round of Matches – Opening bouts between beginners and novice competitors in lighter weight categories.
  • 7:15 PM – 8:00 PM: Mid-Event Intermission & Entertainment – Enjoy a highlight reel of the year's training sessions. Light refreshments available at the concession stands.
  • 8:00 PM – 8:45 PM: Main Event Matches – The Lightweight Championship and Heavyweight Championship bouts.
  • 8:45 PM – 9:00 PM: Closing Ceremony & Award Presentation – The winners will be announced and awarded their championship belts, with special recognition for Best Boxer, Most Improved Boxer, and Best Sportsmanship.

Boxing Categories

  • Lightweight Division: Competitors weighing up to 60kg (132 lbs).
  • Middleweight Division: Competitors weighing between 60kg and 75kg (132–165 lbs).
  • Heavyweight Division: Competitors weighing over 75kg (165 lbs and above).
  • Special Mixed Gender Match: A demonstration match between our female and male competitors to showcase gender equality in sports.

Ticket Information

Admission: The event is free for all students, staff, and faculty members with a valid school ID. Guest tickets for non-student family members or friends are available for a small fee of $5.

Concessions: Refreshments and snacks will be available for purchase at the event. Support the Boxing Club by enjoying snacks like popcorn, chips, and drinks.

Health & Safety Measures

  • All participants will be equipped with proper safety gear, including headgear, mouthguards, and gloves.
  • Medical staff will be on-site for any emergencies or injuries.
  • All spectators are requested to maintain a safe distance from the boxing ring and adhere to the school’s code of conduct.

Why Attend?

  • Support your peers: Cheer on your friends and classmates as they showcase their boxing skills and compete in a friendly but competitive environment.
  • Inspiration & Motivation: Watching these athletes give their all will inspire you to push yourself further in your own pursuits.
  • Exciting Entertainment: High-energy bouts, competitive fighters, and an unforgettable night of sports action.

Important Notes

  • Seating: Seating is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Arrive early to get the best seats.
  • Attire: Casual schoolwear is acceptable. Please avoid wearing hats or oversized clothing that may obstruct your view.
  • Social Media: Share your experience on social media with the hashtag #BoxingChampionship2025.

Contact Information

For further inquiries or to learn more about the event, please contact the Boxing Club President at bcp.edu or visit the school’s sports department.
